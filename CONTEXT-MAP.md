# Context Map

## Contexts

- [PCG Analyzer](./CONTEXT.md) — detects heart sounds in phonocardiogram recordings and derives a BPM/time belief
- [Blender BPM Tool](./Blender%20BPM%20tool/CONTEXT.md) — authors and exports synthetic biometric time-series curves in Blender, for use as fake/demo data

## Relationships

- **Blender BPM Tool → PCG Analyzer**: none today. The Blender tool's CSV exports are standalone fake data; they are not consumed by the analyzer's pipeline.
